## What is not graded, and why

- **Importing into the Postman app.** The app is not scriptable without a signed-in workspace, and an
  import there syncs to Postman's cloud. The schema, the importer differential and the CLI run cover
  what an import reads; a person confirms the app import once per release (see `docs/releasing.md`).
- **Postman's authored templates.** The published copies are forks in individual users' workspaces,
  whose licence and fidelity to Postman's originals cannot be established. The importer differential
  is the reference that comes from Postman itself.
